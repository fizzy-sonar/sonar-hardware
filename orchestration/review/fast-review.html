02 / TWO-MINUTE RISK REVIEW

Your FPGA concern is justified.

1

The fallback is not independent.

The SRAM recorder receives only frames accepted by the USB stream. Fill the stream FIFO and the snapshot tap stops too. A small-FIFO simulation reproduces it. Read the reproduction.

2

There is no integrated “fire → capture” command yet.

The hardware top holds TX start at zero. There is no commanded, timestamped echo measurement to validate. T-023 must connect control, capture, fault handling and a deterministic sample/TX time reference. Source.

3

Simulation does not close the electrical timing budget.

We still need complete PDM/FT232H/SRAM I/O constraints, a Vivado build, resource usage, timing and clock-domain-crossing reports. Stubbed Xilinx primitives are not placed hardware. A clean pin list is not timing closure. Current XDC.

Show the data path and the exact buffer arithmetic

Calculated: 24 channels × 3.072 MHz ÷ 8 = 9.216 MB/s; at 4.8 MHz, 14.4 MB/s. The current 16,384 × 24-bit FIFO holds 49,152 bytes: about 5.33 / 3.41 ms without draining. This is short-stall tolerance, not a guaranteed USB service interval.

A 512 KiB raw SRAM record spans ~56.89 ms at 3.072 MHz. That is not a 10 m range claim: TX duration, start alignment, blanking and acoustic performance all matter.

Two more risks before this becomes a product

Host software can terminate or distort a live capture.

Transient empty reads are treated as EOF, and finite-record DSP resets across chunks. The existing five synthetic tests pass while the independent probes reproduce both behaviors. T-024 owns the live-pipeline fix. Evidence and commands.

Acoustics and safe TX drive remain unmeasured.

Microphone bandwidth, usable SNR and range are not established by the link-budget model. Keep MA40S4S disabled on the 12 V bridge: duty limiting does not establish compliance with its terminal-voltage rating. T-025 must specify a safe, measured bench setup. Independent review.
