## Gotchas for the next agent
- `pins=` keys are KiCad pin NAMES; unnamed pins fall back to number.
- Multi-symbol `.kicad_sym`: `Symbol(library=..., name="Parent")`.
- Strip `Sim.*` properties from vendored KiCad symbols or Component()
  tries to resolve `${KICAD9_SYMBOL_DIR}/Simulation_SPICE.sp` and dies.
- Net names: no `.`; no duplicate `Net("x")` literals (bind to a variable).
- `datasheet=`/`description=` are Component kwargs, not `properties` keys.
- `pcb fmt` takes ONE path. `pcb new board` requires a fake repo URL and
  inits a nested .git (deleted it).
