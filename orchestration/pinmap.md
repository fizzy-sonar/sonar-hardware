## Provenance and audit history (read this before trusting any pin)

- 2026-08-25 (authoring, offline sandbox): the pio->package-pin table was transcribed
  from memory of the Digilent master XDC. The transcription got every net onto a
  physically valid package pin, but assumed the 44 GPIOs were `pio1..pio44` on DIP
  positions 1..44 and invented power pins at positions 45-48.
- 2026-08-26 (second-agent audit, also offline): "confirmed" the table from model
  recall and even "resolved" the T-008 clock-capable list against it. That was a
  correlated-recall failure: both sessions shared the same wrong assumptions, so the
  confirmation was worthless. Lesson recorded: offline recall cannot gate pins.
- 2026-08-26 (live-source gate, orchestrator fetch): **FAILED the table**. The live
  master XDC GPIO section has 44 entries `pio[01]..pio[48]` SKIPPING pio15/16/24/25,
  and reference manual section 8 states: of the 48 DIP pins, 44 are FPGA digital I/O,
  pins 15/16 are voltage-divided (3.3V->1V) XADC analog inputs (not usable for 3.3V
  digital), pin 24 = VU (module 5 V in/out) and pin 25 = GND (the only module GND);
  there is NO 3V3 pin on the DIP. The old table sat every row from position 15 onward
  on the wrong DIP position (+2 for rows 15-21, +4 for rows 22-44) and put FT_D2/FT_D3
  on the VU/GND positions.
- Fix applied here (single source = the generator PIN TABLE): pure position/label
  re-map N->N (1-14), N->N+2 (15-21), N->N+4 (22-44); all net<->package-pin pairings
  unchanged (they were already physically valid; now verified 44/44 vs the live XDC).
  Positions 15/16 = NC with analog note; 24 = VU tied to carrier +5V; 25 = GND; the
  fictional CMOD_3V3 pin and the SJ1/SJ2 rework jumpers are removed.
- The earlier audit's "T-008 CC list is corrupt" resolution is REVERTED: the true
  MRCC/SRCC set computed from the live XDC IO-names is pio3,5,8,18,19 (banks 16/35)
  and pio36,37,38,40,43,46,47,48 (bank 34) - T-008's original list was correct.
  `docs/pdm-capture-contract.md` updated accordingly.
- Clock-critical nets under true labels (all clock-capable): `PDM_CLK_FB` = pio40
  (pkg W4, IO_L12N_T1_MRCC_34), `FT_CLKOUT` = pio47 (pkg U8, IO_L14P_T2_SRCC_34),
  `ETH_REF_CLK` = pio3 (pkg A16, IO_L12P_T1_MRCC_16).

## Board-level decisions (ratified within T-011 scope)

1. **Power strategy (REVISED 2026-08-26)**: the carrier +5V rail (T-012 power tree)
   powers the module through VU (DIP pin 24, the module's 5 V in/out); DIP pin 25 is
   the only module GND. There is NO 3V3 pin on the DIP, so the earlier
   CMOD_3V3/CMOD_VU no-connect + SJ1/SJ2 rework plan is deleted. Do NOT power the
   module from its own USB while carrier +5V is live unless backfeed safety is
   verified. DIP pins 15/16 are XADC analog-only (3.3V->1V divider): NC. FT232H
   breakout self-powered from its own USB (its 5V pin is NC). Pico 2 self-powered.
2. **Contention rule**: exactly one capture platform at a time - Cmod in J40 *or*
   Pico 2 ribbon on J42, never both (both drive PDM_CLK_SRC/PDM_CLK_EN).
3. **Clock architecture**: FPGA generates PDM_CLK_SRC (MMCM from on-module 12 MHz;
   3.072 MHz v1, parameterized for the 4.8 MHz ICS alternate); CDCLVC1112 on the array
   sheet returns PDM_CLK_FB on clock-capable pio40 (MRCC_34, pkg W4); the IDDR samples
   from the returned clock per the capture contract. PDM data lines pio10-14,17-23
   sit together on bank 35.
4. **DNP Ethernet**: LAN8720A + HR911105A MagJack + 50 MHz osc on 10 spare pins;
   strap-resistor values deferred to populate time (Artix-7 config-time pull-ups can
   override PHY internal strap pulls).
5. **PDM data line mapping follows the capture contract exactly** (D0=CH00/CH01=M00/M10
   ... D11=CH22/CH23=M43/M44); T-020 may not renumber channels.
