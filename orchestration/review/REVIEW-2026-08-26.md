The T-011 live-source repair is the strongest work in the day's set and I could not
break it. Two independent defects sit next to it: the Cmod A7 module is **not
connected to any power rail** in the netlist, and the TX amplitude cap that
`tx-limits.md` presents as the transducer over-voltage protection **does not bound the
drive it claims to bound**. Both are cheap to fix and neither is on the critical path
for CP-BM, so this is not "stop everything" — but neither should be carried into a
layout or a bring-up.

# BLOCKER

## B1. The Cmod A7 is not connected to power — `+5V` is an orphan net

`digital.kicad_sch` drives DIP pin 24 (VU) from a KiCad `power:+5V` symbol
(`scripts/gen_digital_sheet.py:77`, symbol `#PWR424` at
`sonar-v1-pcb/digital.kicad_sch:10709`). The T-012 power tree names its mux output
`5V` (a plain global label on the top sheet), **not** `+5V`. KiCad treats these as two
different nets, and the exported netlist proves it:

```
+5V:  1 node   -> ['J40.24']                                   <-- the Cmod VU pin, alone
5V : 14 nodes  -> [... L2.1, Q1.2, Q2.2, U35.3, U61.3, TP5.1, TP80.1 ...]
```

So the module gets nothing. Worse, the defect is **actively masked from ERC**: a
PWR_FLAG (`#FLG403`, `gen_digital_sheet.py:461-462`,
`sonar-v1-pcb/digital.kicad_sch:15861`) was placed on `+5V` back when the power tree
"was not yet in the schematic", and it still declares the net driven. That is why
`/digital/` reports 0 ERC violations on a rail with one pin on it.

**Fix:** make the digital sheet use the same net the power tree produces (rename one
side; `+5V` as a power symbol on the power sheet is the cleaner direction), then delete
the now-lying `#FLG403` PWR_FLAG. Re-run ERC and confirm `J40.24` lands on a net with
the buck/boost inputs on it.

**While you're there — the backfeed question is unresolved and it is not a corner
case.** `pinmap.md:136-138` says "Do NOT also power the module from its own USB while
carrier +5V is live unless backfeed safety is verified." But the architecture *requires*
simultaneous USB: the Cmod's own USB is how you load the bitstream, and D012's snapshot
drain goes out over that same USB (`gateware/constraints/sonar_cmod_a7.xdc:131-133`).
Pin 24 is hard-wired to the rail with no series element, jumper, or diode. Put a DNP 0Ω
/ 2-pin jumper in that feed so you can choose, and say in `rails.md` which way v1 ships.

## B2. `MAX_AMPLITUDE=187` does not bound the TX drive — the PWM carrier is *below* the tone

`tx-limits.md:34-36,50-57` derives the MA40S4S amplitude ceiling from a duty-`d` chopped
square wave: `(2·VM/π)(1−cos(πd)) ≤ 12.73 → d ≤ 0.732 → amplitude ≤ 187/256`. That
derivation assumes the chopping happens *within* each half-cycle.

It does not. In `gateware/rtl/tx_nco_pwm.sv:38,85`, `pwm_count` is a free-running 8-bit
counter that is never re-aligned to the NCO phase, so the PWM period is fixed at
2^8 / 12 MHz = **21.33 µs (46.875 kHz)** while a 40 kHz half-cycle is **12.5 µs**. The
carrier is longer than the thing it is supposed to chop, and the two beat.

I ran the exact RTL arithmetic (PHASE_BITS=24, `phase_increment=55924`, `amplitude=187`)
over 200 k clocks:

```
per-half-cycle duty  min=0.540  max=1.000  mean=0.730  (target 0.730)
half-cycles at duty >= 0.99: 208 of 1328   (15.7%)
```

The *mean* duty is exactly the intended 0.730 — which is why an averaged check would
pass — but **one half-cycle in six is driven at essentially 100% duty**, i.e. the full
24 Vpp bridge output, into a part rated 20 Vpp. The safety cap is a mean, not a bound.

**Fix:** reset `pwm_count` on every NCO half-cycle (MSB edge), or make the PWM period an
exact integer submultiple of the half-period, or drop the PWM approach and set the drive
voltage in hardware. Then re-derive the envelope.

**Mitigating fact, stated plainly:** the shipped `sonar_top` hard-ties
`tx_i.start = 1'b0` and `tx_nsleep = tx_active` (`sonar_top.sv:166,175`), so the bridge
is asleep and nothing transmits today. This is a blocker for the *envelope document and
the first TX bring-up*, not for the current bitstream.

# SHOULD-FIX

## S1. Re-running the generator silently deletes the SRAM/UART constraints
`gateware/constraints/sonar_cmod_a7.xdc:92` says "Regenerating the XDC must keep this
section", but `write_xdc()` (`scripts/gen_digital_sheet.py:617`, called unconditionally
at `:803`) opens the file `'w'` and writes only its own 87 lines. I confirmed by
regenerating into a temp file: **all 47 lines of the manual append (30 SRAM pins +
uart_rxd_out) are gone.** `sim/check_ports.py` does catch the resulting bijection break,
so it is not silent *if* someone runs `make test` — but the destructive edit lands in
the working tree first. Make `write_xdc` read the existing file and preserve everything
from the `MANUAL APPEND` marker onward.

## S2. `rails.md` still describes the pre-repair power plan (root cause of B1)
`orchestration/rails.md:13-14,19-20` lists the Cmod as "SJ2 DNP option (Cmod VU)" /
"SJ1 DNP option (Cmod 3V3)" and states "Cmod A7 / FT232H / Pico 2 are self-powered from
their own USB (T-011 decision)". `pinmap.md:133-139` explicitly **deleted** that plan
("the fictional CMOD_3V3 pin and the SJ1/SJ2 rework jumpers are removed"; carrier +5V
powers the module through VU). Two authoritative docs now disagree about who powers the
FPGA. Consequence beyond the wording: the 5 V rail row has **no current allocation for
the Cmod module at all**, so the 900 mA USB-C budget was never checked against it.

## S3. `tx-limits.md:20` puts TX_NSLEEP on the wrong pio
`| — | TX_NSLEEP | nSLEEP (3) | pio44 / V8 |`. Package pin V8 is correct, but V8 is
**pio48** (`pinmap.md:111`); pio44 is U3 = `ETH_RXD1`. Harmless electrically, but this is
precisely the class of transcription slip that caused the T-011 failure, and it is in the
file `sonar_top.sv:19` cites as authoritative for the TX mapping.

## S4. `tx-limits.md:37` states the wrong DRV8876 idle behaviour
"Idle/reset/reject: IN1=IN2=0 → both half-bridges low-side, transducer at 0 V. Safe."
In the DRV8876's IN1/IN2 (PWM) interface, `00` is **coast** (outputs Hi-Z / bridge
disabled); `11` is brake (both low-side on). This matters during a burst, not at idle:
every PWM off-interval leaves a resonant piezo *floating* rather than damped, so the
ringing element drives the OUTx nodes into the body diodes. The idle claim happens to
hold anyway via `tx_nsleep`, but the stated mechanism is wrong and the burst behaviour
is not what the envelope math assumes. Confirm against the datasheet alongside the
existing PMODE/IMODE CP-C items.

## S5. Amplitude trimming cannot fix an over-voltage at J50
`tx-limits.md:55-57` says "verify differential Vpp at J50 with a scope at CP-E bring-up
and trim down if the measured waveform exceeds 20 Vpp." Duty-chopping a 12 V H-bridge
never reduces the *terminal* excursion: OUT1/OUT2 swing 0↔12 V, so the transducer sees
24 Vpp for any non-zero duty. If Murata's 20 Vpp is a terminal-voltage rating (it is
phrased as one at `tx-limits.md:47`), the only real remedies are a lower VM setpoint, a
series/step-down element, or a different transducer. Say which one v1 uses.

## S6. Reset and `btn[0]` cross clock domains unsynchronized
`sonar_top.sv:78-80` builds `reset` from a **sysclk**-domain POR shift register OR'd with
the raw `btn[1]` pin, then fans it into the `pdm_clk_fb` (3.072 MHz), `ft_clkout`
(60 MHz) and `sram_clk` (48 MHz) domains as a synchronous reset
(`sonar_top.sv:123,126,140-141`). Nothing synchronizes it per domain, so release is
non-simultaneous and metastable in three domains — which is exactly the hazard the
`async_fifo`'s paired `wr_reset`/`rd_reset` are sensitive to. Same for `btn[0]` fed raw
into `sram_snapshot.start` at `:140` (the module does sync it internally at
`sram_snapshot.sv:234-247`, but it is an unbounced mechanical button; expect multiple
triggers). Add a per-domain async-assert/sync-release reset synchronizer.

## S7. The hardware sampling path has no functional simulation
`gateware/sim/xilinx_7series_stubs.sv:21-22` stubs `IDDR` as `assign Q1 = INIT_Q1;` —
a constant. The `-DXILINX` step in `gateware/Makefile:61-64` is `-t null`, i.e.
elaboration only. So every TB exercises the `else` branch of `pdm_ddr_sampler.sv:61-96`,
and the branch that actually gets synthesized (`SAME_EDGE_PIPELINED`, `:19-60`) is never
executed. The same is true of `pdm_clock_7series.sv` in its entirety — it is not in the
Makefile's `RTL` list, and `tb_clock_tx` checks only integer divider arithmetic
(`sim/tb_clock_tx.sv:84-88`), not a waveform. The "12/12 PASS" headline is accurate but
covers less of the shipped logic than it reads like. Plan an xsim/UNISIM equivalence run
on the Vivado host and say so in the ticket.

## S8. `pdm_clk_src` output-enable is not synchronized to the clock it gates
`pdm_clock_7series.sv:89-92` feeds `buffer_oe` (sysclk domain, from
`pdm_clock_startup`) straight into the ODDR `D1` clocked by `selected_clock`
(1.536/3.072 MHz). A metastable capture there lands directly on the mic array clock and
the CDCLVC1112 input. The BUFGCTRL mux select (`:82-87`) has the same shape — `S0`/`S1`
are combinational complements of a sysclk-domain signal, whereas Xilinx wants each
select registered in its own `I0`/`I1` domain. Both are masked in practice because the
FSM only switches while `buffer_oe` is low, but the OE transition itself is not covered.

## S9. FT232H `SIWU#` is left floating
`FT_SIWU` has exactly one node in the netlist (`J41.15`) — the breakout's C4 pin with
nothing on it. `sonar_top.sv:129` deliberately leaves `ft_siwu_n` unconnected because
the DIP budget is full. A floating FTDI control input is not a safe default; add a 10 k
pull-up to +3.3V on the digital sheet (it costs one 0603, not a pin).

## S10. Coupon order package is not self-consistent — you would order the wrong quantity
- `order-package.md:6,12-13`: 16 mics per MPN ("4 coupons worth").
- `order-package.md:24`: 5 boards per variant.
- `order-package.md:31`: "SMD both variants fully assembled".
Fully assembling 5 boards needs 20 mics per variant, not 16. Same arithmetic on
`order-package.md:14`: 8 clock buffers for 10 boards. Either drop to 4 boards/variant or
raise the part quantities; right now JLC will short-build.

## S11. `100 nF C0G/NP0 0603` (×8 per coupon) is not a buyable part
`order-package.md:42`, `coupons/mic-bakeoff/generate_coupon.py:547,586-589`. C0G/NP0 in
0603 tops out around 10–22 nF across the major vendors; 100 nF C0G needs 1206/1210 or
larger. The "(NOT X5R/X7R)" instruction makes the line unfillable as written, and DFM
will bounce it. Decide: X7R at 0603, or C0G in a bigger case with the board space to
match.

# NIT

1. **The pin gate depends on a file in `/tmp`.** `scripts/check_pinmap_vs_xdc.py:35`
   defaults to `/tmp/cmod-a7-master.xdc`. It is the single best artifact produced today
   and it stops working after a reboot. Commit the fetched XDC (or a SHA-256 of it) under
   `orchestration/` so the gate is reproducible without network.
2. **Zero spare DIP I/O.** `pinmap.md:51-52` books 44/44. Ten of those are the DNP
   Ethernet block, so there is de-facto slack, but there is no pin for a control plane —
   which is exactly why `sonar_top.sv:11-17` has to tie off TX start, capture status and
   the snapshot trigger. Worth a conscious decision, not a discovery at bring-up.
3. **`discard_samples` is dead.** Generated by `pdm_clock_startup.sv:19`, wired to a wire
   in `sonar_top.sv:83`, never read. Harmless (the `capture_enable` edge does the job) but
   it reads like implemented behaviour.
4. **`check_coupons.sh` exits 1 on this machine.** T-016's ticket
   (`tickets/T-016-...md:242`) and `STATUS.md:46` both cite "exit 0" as the verification
   of record. I get `SUMMARY: 1 failing steps` / exit 1 — `ruff format --check` wants to
   reformat 4 files under ruff 0.14.0. Everything else in the script passes (ERC 4/4
   waivers per variant, geometric verify, fab export, analysis selftest). Almost certainly
   a ruff-version drift; ruff is not pinned in `pyproject.toml`. Pin it, or the cited
   evidence keeps rotting.
5. **FT232H data bus floats when idle.** `ft245_sync_tx.sv:33` / `sonar_top.sv:132`
   tri-state `ft_d` whenever a byte is not being presented, and `ft_oe_n` is tied high so
   the FT232H never drives either. Since the core is write-only, just keep the FPGA
   driving.
6. **25 % of the stream FIFO is padding.** `pdm_stream_core.sv:127` stores `{8'h00,
   frame}` in a 32-bit FIFO. Documented (`STREAM_FORMAT.md`), and the wire format is
   correctly 3 bytes — it just costs 128 Kib of BRAM for nothing.
7. **Divided clocks come out of fabric flip-flops into BUFGs**
   (`pdm_clock_7series.sv:68-78`). Unavoidable here — 3.072 MHz needs a ÷250 from the
   768 MHz VCO and `CLKOUT*_DIVIDE` caps at 128 — but Vivado will comment on it, and a
   cascaded PLLE2 is the textbook alternative. Decide on the host, don't be surprised.
8. **`rails.md:24`** flags that `sonar.kicad_pcb` still carries footprints from the seven
   deleted sheets. True, and it means the current PCB is not a valid starting point for
   layout until T-010 resyncs.

# OK (checked, no action)

- **The T-011 repair itself.** Position remap, package pins, IO names, bank numbers and
  clock-capable flags agree 44/44 with the live upstream XDC, and all four artifacts
  (generator, `pinmap.md`, XDC, `digital.kicad_sch`) are consistent with each other.
  The provenance narrative in `pinmap.md:12-41` accurately describes what went wrong.
- **SRAM constraints** are byte-identical to the Digilent master XDC's Cellular RAM
  section, and none of the 30 pins collides with the 44 DIP pins.
- **DRV8876 hookup** (`20kHz-h-bridge.kicad_sch`, verified via netlist): VM=+12V,
  charge pump C284 47 nF CPH–CPL and C285 1 µF VCP–VM, VREF=+3.3V, IPROPI 1.69 k to GND,
  IMODE 10 Ω to GND, nFAULT 10 k pull-up, OUT1/2 → R173/R174 0Ω → J50. Snubber R175/C288
  correctly flagged `dnp=yes`. Whole DNP Ethernet block correctly flagged DNP.
- **Power tree topology**: dual ideal-diode OR (VUSB / VXT60 → `5V`), AP63301 buck
  (31.6 k/10 k → 3.33 V), FB2 → `3V3_MIC` with 10 µF + 1 µF and TP86, TPS55340 boost
  (86.6 k/10 k → ≈11.9 V) with EN pulled to +3.3V for the documented sequencing. Power
  sheets are ERC-clean. Mic-rail allocation ≥100 mA is stated and the bench gate
  (`runbook.md` §3: ≤80 mA steady, <100 mA peak) is consistent with it.
- **`async_fifo`**: standard Gray-pointer CDC, 2FF both directions, `ASYNC_REG` applied,
  full/empty both conservative, full asserts after exactly DEPTH writes. I could not
  construct an overflow or a phantom-read case.
- **`sram_snapshot`**: I traced the double-outstanding-read case (`request_pending` and
  `next_pending` both set) and FIFO ordering keeps it correct. Header CRC covers bytes
  0..39 exactly, payload CRC covers the truncated window honestly, `byte_addr` fits 19
  bits at the default window.
- **Wire-format contract holds end to end**: `stream_header.sv` (SNR1, 32 B, flags
  0x0007), `sram_snapshot.sv` (SNP1, 48 B, flags 0), `host/sonar_host/packet.py:17-27`
  (`<4sBBHIQIHHIIIII` = 48, `<4sBBHIQIHHI` = 32, header CRC over `[:40]`), and
  `firmware/pico-snapshot/include/snapshot_protocol.h:9-15` all agree, including
  3 bytes/frame and bit *i* = CH *i*. **No contract drift found.**
- **`uart_tx`**: 8-N-1 bit order and framing correct; 48 MHz / 417 = 115,108 baud
  (−0.08 % vs 115200).
- **`pdm_clock_startup`** ordering is right: `capture_enable` rises only after the buffer
  is re-enabled and the 10 ms settle completes, and its 50 ms + 10 ms matches
  `runbook.md` §0.5.
- **347 project ERC violations** are entirely on the legacy `eight_transducer_array*` /
  `rx_amp_*` / `quad_rx_pre_amp` sheets. Every sheet this batch touched is at zero.

# What Joshua should eyeball himself

Ordered by "cheapest thing that would embarrass us most".

1. **(2 min) Open `digital.kicad_sch`, click DIP pin 24, and confirm which net it lands
   on.** Then click the buck input on `power_supply.kicad_sch`. If the two names differ,
   B1 is real and the FPGA has no power. This is a one-word fix and it is the single most
   consequential thing in this review.
2. **(5 min) Decide the Cmod power strategy and write it into `rails.md`:** carrier-5 V
   through VU, or module self-powered from USB with VU left open. You cannot have both
   without knowing whether VU backfeeds the USB port — and you *will* have USB plugged in,
   because that is how the bitstream and the snapshot drain get there. A DNP jumper in the
   feed buys you the choice for ~nothing.
3. **(10 min) MA40S4S datasheet, Table 1: is the 20 Vpp rating a terminal-voltage limit
   or a fundamental-amplitude limit?** Everything in `tx-limits.md` hangs off that one
   reading. If it is terminal voltage (I believe it is), no amount of duty-chopping
   complies and you need a lower VM or a step-down before first TX.
4. **(10 min) DRV8876 datasheet, three straps at once, since you will have it open:**
   PMODE polarity (does high really select IN1/IN2?), IMODE = 10 Ω-to-GND mode name, and
   the IN1/IN2 truth table for `00` (coast vs brake — S4). These are already on the CP-C
   list; doing them in one sitting closes S4 and the two open items in `tx-limits.md:25-27`.
5. **(5 min) TI CDCLVC1112PWR datasheet vs `BUFFER_PIN_MAP` in
   `coupons/mic-bakeoff/generate_coupon.py`.** T-016's own README calls this the one
   UNVERIFIED item, and it gates the coupon order. Nobody can close it but you (offline
   agents already produced one correlated-recall failure on exactly this kind of lookup).
6. **(5 min) Fix the coupon quantities before you click buy** (S10) and decide the
   100 nF dielectric (S11). Both are in `order-package.md`; both cost you a re-order if
   caught at DFM instead of now.
7. **(10 min) Run `kicad-cli pcb drc` unsandboxed** on `sonar-v1-pcb` and on both coupons.
   It has SIGABRT'd in every agent sandbox all week, so DRC is the one check with *zero*
   real coverage across T-012, T-013, T-016 and T-011.
8. **(3 min) Look at DIP pin 25 and think about layout.** It is the module's only ground
   return for 12 PDM lines plus a 60 MHz 8-bit FT232H bus. Nothing to fix in the
   schematic — but it should shape the ground pour and the J40/J41 placement before
   routing starts.
