## Provenance and audit status

This machine had **no network access** during authoring. The pio->package-pin mapping
is transcribed from memory of the Digilent `Cmod-A7-Master.xdc` and Cmod A7 reference
manual, cross-anchored to the clock-capable pio list documented in
`docs/pdm-capture-contract.md` (which T-008 fetched from the live master XDC).

- **Double-sourced clock-capable pins** (both the recalled mapping and the T-008 list
  agree): **pio3, pio5, pio8, pio36, pio43**. All three clock-critical nets are assigned
  to these: `PDM_CLK_FB`=pio36, `FT_CLKOUT`=pio43, `ETH_REF_CLK`=pio3.
- **Known discrepancy to resolve in the audit**: the T-008 list also calls pio18/19/37/38/40/
  46/47/48 clock-capable, while the recalled mapping puts MRCC/SRCC on pio16/17/32/33/34/39/42/44
  and treats DIP positions 45-48 as power pins (making pio46-48-as-CC impossible if pio
  numbering equals DIP position numbering). The auditor must establish ground truth from
  the Digilent master XDC + reference manual and regenerate; only pin *assignments* on
  non-clock nets would shuffle if the discrepancy resolves against the recall.
- Also audit: DIP positions of the power pins (45=GND, 46=3V3, 47=VU, 48=GND assumed),
  the 0.1in/15.24mm DIP-48 footprint choice, and the Adafruit FT232H header pin order.

## Board-level decisions (ratified within T-011 scope)

1. **Power strategy**: Cmod A7 self-powered from its own micro-USB; board and module
   share GND only. `CMOD_3V3`/`CMOD_VU` socket pins are no-connect by default;
   SJ1/SJ2 (DNP solder jumpers) are rework options to tie module 3V3<->board +3.3V or
   VU<->board 5V, to be fitted only with T-012 review. FT232H breakout self-powered
   from its own USB (its 5V pin is NC). Pico 2 self-powered.
2. **Contention rule**: exactly one capture platform at a time - Cmod in J40 *or*
   Pico 2 ribbon on J42, never both (both drive PDM_CLK_SRC/PDM_CLK_EN).
3. **Clock architecture**: FPGA generates PDM_CLK_SRC (MMCM from on-module 12 MHz;
   3.072 MHz v1, parameterized for the 4.8 MHz ICS alternate); CDCLVC1112 on the array
   sheet returns PDM_CLK_FB on clock-capable pio36; the IDDR samples from the returned
   clock per the capture contract. PDM data lines pio10-21 sit together on bank 35.
4. **DNP Ethernet**: LAN8720A + HR911105A MagJack + 50 MHz osc on 10 spare pins;
   strap-resistor values deferred to populate time (Artix-7 config-time pull-ups can
   override PHY internal strap pulls).
5. **PDM data line mapping follows the capture contract exactly** (D0=CH00/CH01=M00/M10
   ... D11=CH22/CH23=M43/M44); T-020 may not renumber channels.
